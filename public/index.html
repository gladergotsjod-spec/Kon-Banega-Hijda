How to play

  1. Up to 8 players join the same room.
  2. Everyone secretly chooses a number from 1–100. Multiple players may choose the same number.
  3. The server randomly chooses the first turn.
  4. On your turn, cancel an opponent's number.
  5. If you hit someone's secret number, that player is SAFE 🛡️ and cannot become HIJDA.
  6. If you miss, nobody is eliminated and the turn moves on.
  7. The last player remaining LOSES and is declared HIJDA 😈. Everyone who became SAFE is a winner.
